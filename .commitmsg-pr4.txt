M13.9.2, partly. A department administrator already makes an agent in the same flow as a Super Admin (the
builder routes), so what the leaf adds is that they can choose only what their department holds. The form
the builder writes in is now served per reader: a reader whose install authority is held in one department
and not over everything gets the same form with the tool lists (`allowed_tools`, `required_tools`) and the
connector list cut to an enum of what their own reach admits, and a sentence saying why; a Super Admin gets
the form whole. "Holds" is `brain.gate.catalogue.project`'s answer, the one place that decides which tools
an entitlement admits, asked over every registered tool, and a connector is held when a tool of its source
is. A department holding nothing is served a list with no room in it, because JSON Schema refuses an empty
enum.

**This narrows the offer and not the gate, on purpose.** An authored list is intent and never
authorisation (M20.1.5): a name typed past the form still cannot reach what its author does not hold, and a
publish reaching further than its author waits for a second person who holds it. Refusing the name at save
would change a decided behaviour for every builder, so it is a question for the owner rather than something
decided here. Not built: the same cut for skills (the library is one company-wide object and which skills a
department holds depends on what each skill's tools need) and for knowledge (it is not a section of the form,
it is set on the agent's own page behind that page's reach). M13.9.2 is IN PROGRESS for that reason.
Rejected: offering the cut to everybody, which would stop a Super Admin who was deliberately not granted a
department's data from naming that department's tools in a draft a colleague who does hold them approves.

M20.5.3, partly. Behind the switch `describe_an_agent` (ships off), New agent offers "Describe it": the
person writes what the agent is for, a draft of their own is started, and the co-author is asked for its
name, summary, instructions, skills and connectors. The answer is the co-author's own proposal, so it is
not applied: the Write step opens on it with each suggestion beside what the draft holds, none ticked, and
taking is the existing take, by name. The draft is saved before the model is asked, so a failed call leaves
a draft to ask again from rather than nothing, and a process with no model, a person who may not build, a
blank request and the switch off are all refused before any draft exists. What is not drafted is said in the
answer and on the page (the tools and permissions, the knowledge, the channels, how closely it is watched),
because the co-author writes words and never reach (`THE_COAUTHOR_WRITES_WORDS_AND_NEVER_REACH`), so a short
proposal is never read as a finished agent, and "within the person's ceiling" is true by construction: the
draft reaches nothing until its author names something. A procedure is not drawn either. Whether describing
may itself count as taking, and whether the co-author may propose tools and knowledge from within the
person's ceiling, are questions for the owner and change a rule the co-author module argues for.
