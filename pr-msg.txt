Install checks for the company estate and activity, a department head's pace and coverage, role nominations and a member's own export

Six M33 role-surface leaves were built and wired with no install check naming them, so the owner's install could never close them. Each check asks the leaf's own route function as reserved people holding the grants their role means, inside the check's rolled-back transaction, in acceptance_a and acceptance_b.

- M33.1.1.1: a Super Admin is shown an agent and a document of each department, department, person and kind narrow to exactly what they name, and a department's reader is shown their own rows only and answered alike for another department and one that owns nothing.
- M33.1.1.2: company activity narrows to a department's members and to a person, and a reader without the Activity screen is refused.
- M33.2.1.3 and M33.2.1.4: the person who leads a department is shown its day ceiling against the part of the day gone and the knowledge it holds; a member, another department's lead and a department that does not exist get the one refusal. The check says NOT_RUN where the install has no currency, because the product withholds a pace over no recorded cost, and the generic run test pops it as such.
- M33.1.2.3: a nomination is confirmed only by a third person holding the grant decision; the nominator, the nominee and another department's admin are refused.
- M33.3.1.3: a member exports their own conversation, the export is recorded in their name, and a colleague is answered as for a conversation that does not exist.

M33.1.1.3 is not named: nothing on an install writes a company ceiling, so the company budget half of the leaf has no writer (company_routes says so itself).

Every check is shown failing with the product broken in the way it would plausibly break (a lens dropped in both places it is applied, a reach dropped, everybody made a head, the confirmation authority given to all, an export with no record).
