The typical approval wait and the push verdict are moved to READY FOR TESTING, with a mutation table over their guards

Owner decision 222 A: the install may measure how long approvals wait and show only the typical
figure. Nearly all of it was already on main and is reused unchanged: migration 0227's view
gate.approval_wait (raised_at and decided_at only), ops/approval_wait.py (waits_since,
typical_wait, push_is_wanted, worded), console/installation.approval_wait_facts,
install_routes._approval_wait_facts, the install check ops/acceptance_checks_approval_wait.py and
tests/unit/test_approval_wait.py. What was missing, and is added here, is the route: GET /install
tested with the wait facts in it (tests/unit/test_install_approval_wait_route.py), and the two
progress notes.

The route tests pin the disclosure and not only the figure: the body is identical whether three
approvals were decided or thirty-one (no count, and no total key); a refused reader gets one
answer whether the process has rows, a failing database or no database, and the database is not
asked for them while an entitled reader's is (so the refusal does not cost a query or depend on
how the deployment is wired); nothing decided and an unreadable view are each unknown with their
own reason and no value, never zero, and the screen still answers; and the statement names exactly
raised_at and decided_at of gate.approval_wait with only a start (about a quarter back) and a bound
on rows bound, so no person is named.
