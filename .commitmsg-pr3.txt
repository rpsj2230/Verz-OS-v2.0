A department's administrator decides for their department and the company waits for a Super Admin, and a publish that costs more than its department allows waits for a colleague

M20.5.1. Before this a department administrator could publish an agent to every department and approve a
knowledge item to the whole company alone, because the agent door, the promotion door and the console's
controls each asked only that the capability be held in any scope, and a grant scoped to one department
satisfies that. `holds_for_level` is the one question now, asked by `approve_promotion`,
`lifecycle.publish` and the console surfaces: the company needs the capability held over everything
(`scope_narrows(Scope.unrestricted(), held)`), a department needs it held over that department when the
caller can name it, and a personal level needs nothing beyond holding the capability. The queue
(`may_approve_publication`) asks the same function, so a department's administrator is offered only the
requests they could decide, and a skill, which is one company-wide object, is decided only by the authority
held over everything (that was already `skill_library.may_review`; the check holds it). Roles imply no
capability here, so "Super Admin" versus "department admin" is the scope an authority is held in, which is
how the leaf's two people differ. Rejected: a role test, because a role is not an authority in this
product and a second source of the answer is the one that drifts.

Existing tests that gave a department-scoped reader a company decision were updated to the rule: the
queue test now uses department-level requests for a department's reader and a new test holds that a
company request goes only to the holder over everything.

M20.5.2, partly. Behind the switch `publish_cost_hold` (ships off), publishing an agent to a department
whose per-run ceiling is below what one run costs waits for the department's administrator, tells the
author both figures in the install's currency, makes no agent until a colleague approves, and the publish
record carries the cost of each version. The department's ceiling is the RUN-period department budget row
in force (`brain.ops.budget_store.in_force`), and a department with none holds nothing. A publish that
waits only for its cost reaches no further than before, so the approver is not asked to hold everything the
agent reaches (`_approvable` skips the reach test when nothing widens): a cost needs an administrator and
not a reader. Both reasons at once say the widening's sentence, which names the person who can publish it.

**Not what the leaf says, in two ways, and both are questions for the owner.** The leaf asks for the median
of rehearsed runs and the measured cost; rehearsals record no cost yet, so the figure is the run's price
from `brain.ops.spend.estimate` over the agent's tier and tool count, the estimator the pre-flight admits a
real run by (`A_PRICED_RUN_IS_NOT_A_MEASURED_ONE`, said in the module and on the progress note). And no
screen writes a department's per-run ceiling yet (M21.1.2), so on an install the hold fires only for a
ceiling written some other way. M20.5.2 is therefore IN PROGRESS and M20.5.1 READY FOR TESTING.
