A person's own Google account can be attached to their personal agent, an auditor is told personal agents exist, and a team agent can never carry either

The owner's direction on needs item 173 (2026-10-12) is that a person connects their own Google
account to their own agent, that nobody else can ask for it, and that a reader who holds the audit
capability may be shown a personal agent for oversight. Three leaves were open for it. This builds
M39.10.3 and M39.10.4 up to the real Google sign-in, which is the owner's step on item 173, and the
part of M39.10.5 that holds under every answer to item 189. It does not decide item 189.

What was found first, and it is the load-bearing change. `read:google_workspace`, the capability a
person needs to be read anything from Workspace, names an entity ("google_workspace") that no
connector provided. `brain.agents.binding.bound_capabilities` keeps a capability on an entity nobody
provides in every ceiling that lists it, so the strip a team agent got in 491629be (bound_connectors)
removed the connector and left the read. A department's agent that named the capability read the
asker's own mail into answers other people read, and the M11.9.12 install check passed BECAUSE of
that: it bound a department's agent and watched it read one person's mailbox. The Google connector
now provides the entity, so the read is bound like every other, and that check binds the owner's own
personal agent instead and asserts a department's agent naming Workspace is handed nothing. This
replaces what the check asserted; it was asserting the leak.

M39.10.4. Attaching is naming the connector on the agent's own list, and a personal agent that names
a connector of its owner's own accounts carries that connector's read in its ceiling, derived from the
one list (agents.model.own_reads). The alternative, also writing the read into the agent's
capabilities, is a second record of the same attachment. The owner attaches and detaches from the
agent's Profile (new route /agents/{id}/own-workspace, confirmed in the page); a colleague, an
administrator and a team agent's page get the 404 an agent never made gets, and a press on a team
agent the reader can see is the one sentence. The connector administrator is deliberately not given
this: an administrator's press is never a person's consent. The write is the existing compare and set
on the connector list (StoredAttachments.bind_connectors), one ledger entry naming the owner and
carrying own_workspace_attached or own_workspace_detached. Behind the switch
personal_agent_own_workspace, which ships off; detaching never needs it.

M39.10.3. Oversight is a view of its own and never a wider audience: widening visible_to would have
made who may see an agent and who may use it the same answer, the conflation AUDIENCE_IS_NOT_AUTHORITY
refuses. GET /agent-oversight tells a reader holding read:audit.agent (the existing capability, in the
scope their grant carries, asked through audit.view.may_audit in the owner's name so a department head's
people-scoped grant covers those people's agents) that another person's personal agent exists, its
name, owner and state, and offers nothing: not on the roster, its page refused as for an agent never
made. No count; every reader gets 200 and the same two fields. Behind personal_agent_oversight (off).
Two leaks found while checking every list follow the owner-only scope: a department or person named
beside a personal audience made the agent findable (visible_to now ignores them at that level), and a
leaver's personal agent was offered for adoption to whoever may adopt agents (transfers_for skips it).
A source-walking test requires every module that reads agents in bulk to call the audience question or
be listed with a reason. NOT done and a question for the owner: the Usage and Spend breakdowns by
agent name every agent to a reader of those screens, personal ones included.

M39.10.5, the part that holds under A, B and C: a team agent can never carry a person's own account
(above). Not built, waiting on item 189: a shared mailbox an administrator connects, and a person's
own mail inside their private conversation only. The leaf stays open.
